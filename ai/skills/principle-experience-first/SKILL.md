# Experience First

The product is the experience. Implementation convenience is not a reason to make the central workflow worse.

- Make every feature, control, and option earn its place.
- Prefer a smaller polished core over a broad rough surface.
- Use a throwaway prototype when feel or interaction cannot be settled from logic alone.
- Cover feedback, error states, accessibility, and transitions appropriate to the surface.
- Remove features that distract from the core loop.

The user may be an end user, CLI operator, library consumer, skill author, or the next maintainer. Name what they will notice and optimize for that outcome without ignoring safety or long-term ownership.

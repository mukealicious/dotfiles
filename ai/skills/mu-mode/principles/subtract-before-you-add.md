# Subtract Before You Add

Remove complexity before building on top of it. A smaller base reveals the essential shape and reduces the cost of the next change.

- Remove dead paths, duplicate decisions, obsolete compatibility code, and references with no novel content when safe.
- Cut scope to observed needs before polishing.
- Do not add speculative features, validators, retries, persistence, or migrations without a real contract that requires them.
- Simplify repeated or conflicting prose instead of adding another instruction layer.
- Keep protections required by external inputs, compatibility, rollout, and repository safety.

Sequence deletion only when its replacement contract is ready. Do not remove existing guidance or behavior before the new owner works and has been verified.
